How to play

  • You are white. Both sides start with two pieces.
  • Click a white piece, then click a highlighted target to move.
  • Capture to score: knight and bishop = 3, rook = 5, queen = 9.
  • After any capture, a new piece spawns on a random empty square.
  • Only legal chess moves. No kings, no check.
  • Highest white score at time-up wins. Best score is saved locally.
