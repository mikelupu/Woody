How to play

  • Kingless (default): both sides have 8 pawns. Lose when you have no pieces left.
  • With kings: standard chess with only pawns and kings; checkmate wins.
  • You are white by default. Set variant, side, and Elo via the gear.
  • Click a pawn, then a highlighted target to move.
