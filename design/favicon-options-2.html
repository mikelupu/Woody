Favicon options — take 2

Five fresh directions. This round leans on real artwork (Cburnett) for the detailed piece, and clean geometric primitives (rectangles, circles, diagonals) for everything else — no more hand-drawn wobbly paths.

The top strip is the size that actually matters: 16 px in a browser tab. If a design doesn't hold up here it doesn't matter how good it looks big.

Traditional

A · Cburnett knight (real artwork)

The actual black Cburnett knight from the piece set already shipping in the app, placed on a cream tile with a soft forest border. This is real professional line-art rather than my previous hand-drawn horror; it holds up at every size ≥ 20 px and is instantly recognisable as chess.

Typographic

B · Bold H monogram

A single bold H in gold on a forest tile. No fake chess garnish this time — just a clean letterform (single path, no rogue rectangles). Reads perfectly at 16 px because it's just three thick strokes. Feels like a small product brand mark.

Modern flat

C · Cream pawn silhouette on forest

A simplified pawn — head, neck, body, base — drawn as clean geometry rather than illustrated. Pawn is the easiest chess piece to render legibly at small sizes because it's near-radial. Modern app-icon feel; still obviously chess.

Pure geometry

D · Chessboard tile

A 2×2 mini chessboard using the exact --light-square and --dark-square tokens from the app, framed by a forest border. Nothing else. No piece, no letter. Says "chess" without picking a piece; matches the palette of the actual boards you play on.

Minimal / abstract

E · Diagonal split with gold rule

The tile is cut diagonally — cream in the upper-right, forest in the lower-left, with a single gold hairline along the cut. A tiny gold square sits in the cream half. Reads as a chess-square abstracted; works as a brand mark even if you didn't know the site.

Once picked: the chosen SVG goes to src/hanging_piece_trainer/static/favicon.svg, gets linked from every template with <link rel="icon" type="image/svg+xml">, and a 32 px-derived favicon.ico gets added for old Safari / Windows.
