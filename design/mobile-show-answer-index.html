Mobile — Show-answer panel options

Three approaches to replace the current fixed-height (380px) shelf that squeezes the board on short phones. Each mockup shows a closed and open state at 390×780; pick the pattern you like and we'll build it into the real page.

Current issue: the details panel snaps to 380px whether the phone is 667px tall (iPhone SE) or 900px tall. On short phones the board is crushed to ~183px and the moves table gets its own nested scrollbar. On tall phones there's dead space and misalignment against the action strip.
